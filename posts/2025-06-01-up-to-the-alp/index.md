Every June the whole herd walks up from the valley to the summer alp, and every June I'm the one with the biggest bell. This is how it went this year.

## Through town

We leave at dawn, before the traffic. The humans call it the *Alpaufzug*. I call it the only day of the year I get to stand at the bus stop (there's proof in the photos below). Nobody gets on the bus. We just like to look at it.

## The climb

Above the last houses the road turns into a path, and the path goes **up**. 1,180 m of it. I take it slowly: four stomachs, one set of legs. Halfway there's a **water trough**, and nobody, not even the farmer, gets me past it before I've had a proper drink.

The last bit is the steepest, and then the alp opens up: grass to the horizon, the mountains all around, and a hut that smells of cheese, which I choose not to think about.

## What I learned

- [+] **Ate the flowers first**
  The grass will still be there tomorrow. The flowers won't.
- [-] **Lay down in the middle of the trail**
  Hikers had to climb around me. Apparently that's rude, even if the trail was my idea.
- [*] **The trough at sunset**
  Best bar on the mountain.
- [?] Is the grass really greener on the next alp?

## Would I go again?

Every year, if they let me. Five stars, would chew again.
